Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They contain per-fold and per-split scores
and summary statistics only. No subject-level data are included: subject identifiers,
subject-level predictions and covariates were removed before release. The underlying
imaging and behavioural data must be obtained from their providers under their own terms.
